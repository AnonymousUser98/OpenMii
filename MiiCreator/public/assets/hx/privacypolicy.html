Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Mii Creator ("we", "us", or "our") handles user privacy, account identifiers, cookies, and application data.

  1. Collection of Information

    We practice strict data minimization and only collect what is necessary to authenticate your account and sync your save data:
    • OAuth Account Identifiers: We do not use password-based accounts. Authentication is handled via third-party providers:
      • Google OAuth: We only request basic identification to obtain your unique Google User ID. We do not request, store, or access your email address, contacts, or private profile data.
      • Discord OAuth: We only receive your unique Discord User ID, username, and public display name. We do not request or store your email address.
    • User-Generated App Data & Archives: We collect and store content you create within Mii Creator on our servers to enable cloud saves and app functionality. You can export or import your data at any time. To protect against data loss, we automatically create periodic snapshot archives when you make changes. Stored data and snapshot archives may be accessed by authorized server administrators solely for technical maintenance, manual data restoration upon request, or ensuring platform security.
    • Technical & Network Logs: When you access our site, basic technical information (such as IP address, browser type, device information, and request headers) passes through our infrastructure and network providers, such as Cloudflare, solely for security filtering, DDoS protection, rate limiting, and basic server performance monitoring.

  2. Use of Information

    Your data and application snapshots are strictly used to:
    • Link your application saves and archives to your unique Google or Discord User ID so you can access your progress across devices.
    • Maintain server-side backup snapshots to assist with manual data recovery, technical debugging, or infrastructure maintenance.
    We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any user data, user-generated content, or snapshots with third parties. We do not run advertisements, serve third-party ad tracking, or send marketing emails. We do not use Google APIs outside the standard login process.

  3. Cookies & Analytics

    We use minimal, strictly necessary technologies to run and secure the site:
    • Session Cookie: A single session cookie used strictly to keep you authenticated while using the app.
    • Security & Cloudflare Services: We use Cloudflare for network security, DDoS protection, and privacy-first web analytics. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies, fingerprinting, or personal tracking. Any security tokens or cookies set by Cloudflare (e.g., bot detection challenges) are strictly necessary for core site security.
    None of these tools are used for advertising, behavioral tracking, or cross-site profiling, and none require pop-up consent banners under privacy regulations.

  4. Security of Information

    We take reasonable administrative and technical steps to protect your personal information and server-stored save files from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

  5. Third-Party Links

    Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of those external sites.

  6. Google API Disclosure

    Mii Creator's use of information received from Google APIs is limited strictly to user authentication and adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  7. Data Deletion & Your Rights

    You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account record, stored application saves, and associated server snapshots at any time.
    Self-Service Account Deletion:
    Because we practice strict data minimization, we do not collect or store email addresses. Stored application data is tied exclusively to your unique Google or Discord OAuth ID. Because an unauthenticated email provides no way to verify account ownership, we cannot process manual deletion requests sent via email.
    To ensure that unauthorized third parties cannot request the deletion of your account, you must initiate the deletion process directly within the application while logged in:
    1. Log into Mii Creator using your Google or Discord account.
    2. Navigate to Settings -> Profile.
    3. Click "Delete Account" to immediately and permanently purge your account and all associated data from our servers.
    If you no longer have access to the underlying Google or Discord OAuth account used to sign up, we cannot authenticate your identity or locate your specific data record, and the account cannot be manually deleted. Upon initiating account deletion, your primary account record and save files are immediately removed from active database storage. Backup snapshots and server archives are permanently overwritten or purged in accordance with our automated backup lifecycle (e.g., within 30 days).

  8. Changes to Privacy Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by updating the policy date on this page.
