About the Project

Mii Creator is a 2024 project created by Austin (kat21) that began as a solo passion project and slowly developed into a large Mii Creation Tool with some of the most expansive features currently available.
Custom hats, accessories, and rendering allow YOU to customize your Miis to your heart's content.

NOTE: We are NOT affiliated with Nintendo!
